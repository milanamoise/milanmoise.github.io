# About Me

I am pursuing a Master of Engineering in Mechanical Engineering and Materials Science at Duke University, with a Robotics Certificate and expected graduation in December 2026. I earned my Bachelor of Science in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering from George Washington University in May 2025.

My interests span robotics, systems engineering, technical consulting, and project management. I enjoy understanding how technical solutions develop from an initial problem through design, integration, and verification.

## Experience

As a Technical Consulting Intern in Siemens Digital Industries, I have worked on a standardized liquid cooling implementation framework supporting more than 50 data center accounts. I have also coordinated development of a Coolant Distribution Unit starter kit through project timelines, milestones, and cross-functional collaboration.
